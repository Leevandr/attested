What a receipt does and does not prove

Verified here, with real cryptography

  • Intel TDX quote signature chains up to Intel's pinned root
  • The enclave's signing key and our nonce sit inside the signed quote
  • The boot log replays to the measured registers
  • NVIDIA's signed verdict for 8 GPUs, bound to the same nonce
  • The receipt's own hashes: change one byte and it fails

Not proven (and labelled that way)

  • Intel TCB status and revocation (needs Intel collateral)
  • That the published software is the audited build
  • Streaming answers (signed by the gateway, not the model)
  • That the answer is correct
  • The browser cannot re-check the secp256k1 response signature; the CLI does
